Job Agent privacy policy

Last updated: 6 October 2026

Job Agent is a personal tool written and used only by Josiah Francis to organise his own job search. It is not offered to anyone else.

What it accesses

With Josiah's own Google sign-in, the tool has read-only access to Gmail (the gmail.readonly permission). It reads only emails carrying the Gmail label "Job Alerts", which are job alert emails from sites such as LinkedIn, Cake and Indeed.

What it stores

From those emails it saves only job details: title, company, location and link. It does not save email bodies, and it does not read or store any other mail. Everything is kept on Josiah's own computer.

What it shares

Nothing. No data is sent to any other person, company or service, sold, or used for advertising or training.

Changing or removing access

Access can be removed at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
